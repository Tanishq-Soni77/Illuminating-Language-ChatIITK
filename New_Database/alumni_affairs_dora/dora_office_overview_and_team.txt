About the Fund
The Arzoo Katiyar Memorial Fund has been created to honour the life, values, and legacy of Arzoo Katiyar - a deeply compassionate human being whose impact continues to live on through the people she touched.
Arzoo believed deeply in sincerity, intellectual honesty, hard work, and kindness. Whether in classrooms, research labs, or hostel corridors, she brought warmth, clarity, and quiet strength into every space she occupied. This fund seeks to carry that spirit forward by supporting students at IIT Kanpur, especially young researchers striving to explore, present, and contribute to the global academic community. More than a memorial, this initiative is a continuation of everything Arzoo stood for: curiosity, generosity, excellence, humility, and care for others.
[Context: IIT KANPUR DORA - OFFICE OVERVIEW, TEAM, HOME AND ALUMNI PAGES > arzoo]
Arzoo Katiyar
September 9, 1990  -  May 30, 2021
Arzoo was born in Hapur, Uttar Pradesh, and raised in Kanpur, where she grew up with a strong sense of discipline, empathy, integrity, and responsibility, qualities that defined her throughout her life.
She completed her B.Tech-M.Tech Dual Degree in Computer Science and Engineering from IIT Kanpur in 2013, and pursued a Ph.D. in Computer Science at Cornell University, where her work focused on natural language processing. She completed her doctorate in 2019, followed by a postdoctoral research position at ASAPP Inc., before joining the Penn State College of Engineering as an Assistant Professor of Computer Science in 2020.
But Arzoo was much more than her remarkable academic achievements.
[Context: IIT KANPUR DORA - OFFICE OVERVIEW, TEAM, HOME AND ALUMNI PAGES > arzoo]
She listened patiently, encouraged generously, and cared deeply. She was fiercely principled yet endlessly warm, sincere yet playful, brilliant yet profoundly humble. Her friends remember her laughter and the comfort of simply being around her. She made people feel supported, understood, and valued.
The impact she left behind continues to shape countless lives through memories, friendships, and mentorship.
The Arzoo Katiyar Memorial Travel Grant
One of the most transformative experiences for a student researcher is the opportunity to present their work before an international academic community - conferences open doors to new ideas, collaborations, mentorship, and confidence.
Envisioned to support deserving students at IIT Kanpur in accessing global research opportunities. Inspired by Arzoo's own academic journey and her commitment to research excellence, the grant encourages young scholars, especially women in STEM, to pursue ambitious research and engage with the global scientific community.
[Context: IIT KANPUR DORA - OFFICE OVERVIEW, TEAM, HOME AND ALUMNI PAGES > arzoo]
Student Eligibility
The grant is primarily intended to support:
Undergraduate students in the 3rd, 4th, or 5th year of their academic program
B.Tech, B.Tech-M.Tech Dual Degree, BS, and BS-MS integrated program students
Students who are first authors of accepted research papers at reputed international conferences
Preference will be given to eligible female students at IIT Kanpur. If the grant cannot be awarded to an eligible female student in a given year, it may be opened to all eligible students, irrespective of gender or first-author status.
Areas of Support
The fund is primarily intended to support students in presenting their research at international conferences. To facilitate this, financial assistance will be provided for:
[Context: IIT KANPUR DORA - OFFICE OVERVIEW, TEAM, HOME AND ALUMNI PAGES > arzoo]
Conference registration fees
Travel and transit expenses (flights, visas, and local transport)
Accommodation during the conference
Academic presentation-related expenses (poster printing, etc.)
Selection Criteria
Applications will be evaluated based on:
Quality and impact of research work
Reputation and relevance of the conference
Academic merit and overall contribution
The Institute will constitute a selection committee to identify deserving recipients.

Prof. Abhay Lalit Deshpande
(MSC2/PHY/1987)
Dr. Deshpande is a Distinguished Professor of Physics at Stony Brook University (State University of NY) and serves as Associate Laboratory Director (ALD) for Nuclear and Particle Physics at US DOE's Brookhaven National Laboratory (BNL). For his research, Prof. Deshpande is involved in experiments at the Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider (RHIC), the Thomas Jefferson National Accelerator Facility (JLab) and Paul Sherrer Institute (PSI) in Switzerland.
Profile
Prof. Abhay Lalit Deshpande received his master's degree in physics from IIT Kanpur in 1987 and subsequently went on to earn his doctorate in Experimental High Energy Physics from Yale University in 1994. Honoring his stellar contributions to the fields of High Energy Particle and Nuclear Physics, IIT Kanpur confers upon Prof. Abhay Lalit Deshpande the Distinguished Alumnus Award of 202l.
[Context: IIT KANPUR DORA - OFFICE OVERVIEW, TEAM, HOME AND ALUMNI PAGES > prof abhay lalit deshpande]
In the past, Prof. Abhay has performed experiments at CERN, Geneva, Switzerland and at DESY in Hamburg, Germany.He was one of the original promoters of the Electron Ion Collider. Since 2017, he has served as the Founding Director at Center for Frontiers in Nuclear Science,and Scientific Director for the Electron Ion Collider being built at Brookhaven National Laboratory. As Electron Ion Collider Director of Science, he helps develop and enhance the scientific scope of the US Electron Ion Collider. At the Center for Frontiers in Nuclear Science, he leads the activities that promote the science and international collaborations for the EIC through collaborative research and mentoring exceptional postdoctoral fellows and graduate students. The Center runs an annual series of international workshops on the science of EIC, a robust postdoctoral fellowship program, a seminar series, a visitors' program and an annual summer school associated with the science of EIC. His research interests are in the fields of particle and nuclear physics and in particular: Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD),precision Electro-Weak & Beyond the Standard Model Physics. He is also passionate about communicating science and promoting the importance of such abilities amongst early career scientists.
[Context: IIT KANPUR DORA - OFFICE OVERVIEW, TEAM, HOME AND ALUMNI PAGES > prof abhay lalit deshpande]
Prof. Deshpande is the recipient of many prestigious awards and honors, such as, Fellowship of the American Association for the Advancement of Science (2021),induction into the NY Distinguished Academy (2021), SUNY Distinguished Professorship (2021), the SUNY Chancellor's Award for Excellence Research & Innovation in 2018; RIKEN (Japan) President's Prize for the Study of Nucleon Spin in 2015; Fellow of the American Physical Society in 2014; National Academy of Science's Education Fellow in Life Sciences in 2013.

Prof. Ashok Gadgil (MSC2/PHY/1973)
Prof. Gadgil is Affiliate (former Faculty Senior Scientist and former Director of the Energy and Environmental Technologies Division) of Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. He is a Distinguished Professor Emeritus of Civil and Environmental Engineering, and Professor of the Graduate School, at University of California Berkeley. Prof. Gadgil specializes in innovative application of engineering, including inventions, for serious problems of low-income communities and people. His work experience includes technical, economic, and policy research on energy efficiency and its implementation, particularly in developing countries. His inventions such as the Berkeley-Darfur Stove (which is being deployed in Darfur by the nonprofit Potential Energy) and UV Waterworks, a technology to inexpensively disinfect drinking water, have been used extensively in developing countries. The 2023 open-access Springer graduate level textbook “Introduction to Development Engineering”, co-edited and co-authored by him, has been downloaded more than 280,000 times as of August 2025.
[Context: IIT KANPUR DORA - OFFICE OVERVIEW, TEAM, HOME AND ALUMNI PAGES > prof ashok gadgil]
Profile
Prof. Gadgil has won numerous awards and honors for his inventions, including the Heinz Award for the Environment (2009), Sustainability Pioneer Award from SAG/SAM of Zurich (2010), The European Inventor Award, and the NCIIA Lifetime Award for teaching innovation (both 2011), and the Zayed Future Energy Prize, and the Lemelson-MIT Global Innovation Award (both 2012). He was elected to the National Academy of Engineering in 2013. In 2014, Prof. Gadgil was inducted into the National Inventors Hall of Fame. The American Physical Society Forum on Physics and Society also conferred upon Prof. Gadgil its Leo Szilard Lectureship Award. Prof. Gadgil holds a Ph.D. in Physics from University of California, Berkeley.
[Context: IIT KANPUR DORA - OFFICE OVERVIEW, TEAM, HOME AND ALUMNI PAGES > prof ashok gadgil]
He is the Laureate of the National Medal of Technology and Innovation, the highest recognition in the US, with the medal presented to him in 2023 by the US President in the White House. IITK conferred upon Prof. Ashok Gadgil the Distinguished Alumnus Award, 2020.

Prof. C. Mauli Agrawal (BT/ME/1982)
Prof.C.Mauli Agrawal is currently the Chancellor of the University of Missouri-Kansas City. Earlier, he served as the Interim Provost and the Vice-President for Academic Affairs at the University of Texas, San Antonio (UTSA). Prof. Agrawal research areas include orthopedic and cardiovascular biomaterials/implants, and he has authored more than 300 scientific publications and has 29 patents in his name. His UTSA bioengineering research group has been responsible for starting three companies in San Antonio.
[Context: IIT KANPUR DORA - OFFICE OVERVIEW, TEAM, HOME AND ALUMNI PAGES > prof c mauli agrawal]
Prof. Agrawal currently serves on the board of trustees of the Southwest Research Institute, and is a member of Biomed SA, the Texas Research Park Foundation and the San Antonio Medical Foundation. He worked closely with the City of San Antonio and Mayor Julian Castro to establish the Texas Sustainable Energy Research Institute at UTSA, which received a $50 million pledge of support from CPS Energy.
Prof. Agrawal is a Fellow of Biomaterials Science and Engineering, the National Academy of Inventors, the American Association for the Advancement of Science, and the American Institute for Medical and Biological Engineering. Additionally, he has also served as the president of the Society for Biomaterials in 2006. He has served on the editorial boards of various scientific journals, including the Journal of Biomedical Materials Research, Tissue Engineering, the Journal of System of Systems, and the Journal of Tissue Engineering and Regenerative Medicine. IITK conferred upon Prof. C. Mauli Agrawal the Distinguished Alumnus Award, 2020.

About Us
The DoRA office plays a vital role in IIT Kanpur's success. It bridges the gap between the institute and its alumni, fostering strong connections. The mission is two-fold: to strengthen these bonds and secure resources to fuel the institute's long-term goals. To professionalize alumni engagement, the institute established the IITK Development Foundation (IITK DF) in 2020, led by CEO Mr. Kapil Kaul. An extended arm of the DoRA office, IITK DF's team works with alumni, corporations, and philanthropists to raise funds.
[Context: IIT KANPUR DORA - OFFICE OVERVIEW, TEAM, HOME AND ALUMNI PAGES > DoRA website common home-page content (appeared on the home ]
Throughout the year, DoRA's team organizes events like reunions, alumni day, and Foundation Day to connect with the accomplished alumni network of IITK. These events span the globe, helping alumni rekindle the bond with their alma mater. IIT Kanpur graduates go on to make significant contributions in academia, industry, entrepreneurship, and government. Their achievements serve as a constant inspiration for current students.
DoRA team's efforts have resulted in the establishment of over 149 scholarships, 54 Faculty Chairs, and numerous awards. The institute's infrastructure has grown significantly, along with the creation of new departments and centres.
IIT Kanpur is committed to continuous improvement. New ventures like the departments of Sustainable Energy Engineering, Cognitive Science, Design, and Space Sciences & Astronomy, Kotak School of Sustainability along with the Gangwal School of Medical Sciences and Technology, have been established. To support these initiatives, DoRA office seeks continued alumni contributions. The institute has seen a remarkable rise in donations, with an ambitious goal of reaching over Rs. 750 crores in the next three years (July 2024 - July 2027). This funding will be crucial in supporting the endeavours of the institute.

The message is clear: let's work together to push boundaries, innovate, and expand IIT Kanpur's reach. We value your unwavering support, creative ideas, and constructive feedback.

Prof. Amey Karkare
