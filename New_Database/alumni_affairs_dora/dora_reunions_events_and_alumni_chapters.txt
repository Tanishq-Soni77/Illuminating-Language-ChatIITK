About FACE
The Faculty Alumni Collaborative Endeavour (FACE) is IIT Kanpur's flagship international academic networking platform, created to strengthen research collaborations across institutions, disciplines, and geographies.
FACE brings together distinguished faculty members, accomplished alumni academicians, research professionals, doctoral scholars, postdoctoral researchers, and industry leaders to exchange ideas, build partnerships, and explore opportunities that advance cutting-edge research and innovation.
More than a symposium, FACE serves as a catalyst for meaningful collaborations, enabling IIT Kanpur's global academic community to connect, mentor, and co-create solutions to some of the world's most pressing challenges.
[Context: IIT KANPUR DORA - REUNIONS, SAMVAD, FACE, IITK SAMANVAY, FOUNDATION DAY AND ALUMNI CHAPTERS (II > face face 2026]
THE JOURNEY CONTINUES
From Stanford to MIT
The inaugural edition of FACE, held at Stanford University in 2025, laid the foundation for a vibrant global academic network, fostering conversations that sparked new collaborations across emerging areas of science, technology, and innovation.
Building on that momentum, FACE 2026 arrives at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), opening the next chapter of this growing international platform dedicated to advancing interdisciplinary research and strengthening IIT Kanpur's global academic engagement.
Norman Dahl, the KIAP Leader Designate, with Dr. P.K. Kelkar.
A RETURN TO A HISTORIC PARTNERSHIP
Where Our Story Began.
Where New Collaborations Begin.
[Context: IIT KANPUR DORA - REUNIONS, SAMVAD, FACE, IITK SAMANVAY, FOUNDATION DAY AND ALUMNI CHAPTERS (II > face face 2026]
FACE 2026 carries special significance as IIT Kanpur returns to one of the institutions that helped shape its foundation.
Between 1962 and 1972, the Kanpur Indo-American Programme brought together MIT and eight leading American universities to help establish IIT Kanpur as a pioneering institution for engineering education, scientific research, and technological innovation in India. The faculty exchanges, laboratories, and curricula born in that decade shaped the institute for generations.
Sixty years on, hosting FACE at MIT celebrates this enduring partnership — and looks ahead to a new era of collaborative research, academic excellence, and global impact.

Foundation Day
Every year on November 2, the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur celebrates its Foundation Day, marking the Institute’s remarkable journey of excellence and impact. On this special occasion, an eminent Chief Guest is invited and the Institute honours its alumni and faculty by conferring its most prestigious recognitions across various categories, including Institute Fellows, the Distinguished Alumni Award, the Satyendra K. Dubey Memorial Award, the Distinguished Service Award, and the Young Alumni Award. These awards celebrate alumni who have made outstanding contributions in their respective fields and have brought pride to their alma mater.
