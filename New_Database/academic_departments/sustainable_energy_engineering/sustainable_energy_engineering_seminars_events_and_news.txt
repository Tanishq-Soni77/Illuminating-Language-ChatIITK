IIT Kanpur to Develop India’s First Sun Simulator for Concentrated Photovoltaic Systems ☀️🔬

IIT Kanpur has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Bengaluru-based NexPV Energy Systems, facilitated by Invariance Automation, a member company of the Research and Technology Park Foundation (Technopark IITK), to design and develop India’s first Sun Simulator for Concentrated Photovoltaic (CPV) systems.

Led by Prof. Ashish Garg, Prof. Laltu Chandra and team from the Department of Sustainable Energy Engineering, IIT Kanpur, the project will enable CPV modules to be tested and characterised at the end of production lines in India, building domestic testing capability for next-generation solar technologies.

[Context: DEPARTMENT OF SUSTAINABLE ENERGY ENGINEERING IIT KANPUR - SEMINARS, EVENTS, WORKSHOPS, NEWS AND > news]
CPV systems use optical lenses to concentrate sunlight onto highly efficient multi-junction solar cells, requiring significantly less cell area than conventional photovoltaic panels. NexPV Energy Systems, led by Mr. Chidananda R Murthy, has developed indigenous CPV technology and is moving towards large-scale manufacturing.

Center of Excellence for Green Hydrogen

We are delighted to share that the Government of Uttar Pradesh has approved the establishment of a Center of Excellence for Green Hydrogen at IIT Kanpur.

[Context: DEPARTMENT OF SUSTAINABLE ENERGY ENGINEERING IIT KANPUR - SEMINARS, EVENTS, WORKSHOPS, NEWS AND > news]
The Centre will be established with the support of the Uttar Pradesh New and Renewable Energy Development Agency (UPNEDA) and EcoGen, strengthening collaborative efforts to advance India's green hydrogen ecosystem.

The Centre will focus on advanced research in green hydrogen production, safe storage, system integration, and industrial applications. It will also promote technology development, pilot projects, industry-academia collaboration, and innovation to accelerate the transition towards clean and sustainable energy.

This landmark initiative is expected to drive the adoption of green hydrogen technologies, support startups, foster indigenous innovation, and contribute significantly towards achieving India's net-zero ambitions.
