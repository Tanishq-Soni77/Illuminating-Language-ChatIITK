If you are a prospective Chemistry faculty looking for an institute to pursue your academic career, then this is the right place for you. The Department of Chemistry at IIT Kanpur welcome applications in all areas of Chemistry. As a faculty of this department you will have good infrastructure and research facilities and also live on a beautiful and green campus with several amenities. The academic environment of IIT Kanpur will invariably get you involved in stimulating discussions with colleagues from the department and the institute and several bright students who will engage and challenge you. The colourful campus life is an added bonus with several extra-curricular activities and events.
