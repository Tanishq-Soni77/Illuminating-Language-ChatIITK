Transforming Healthcare
Where knowledge meets care

The Gangwal School of Medical Sciences and Technology is committed to revolutionizing the Indian healthcare system with cutting-edge medical technologies and serving as a role model for other medical institutes to follow. Our focus is on driving disruptive innovations in diagnostics, treatment, and patient care. We are dedicated to training the next generation of healthcare professionals to lead the way in improving medical practices and advancing patient outcomes. With state-of-the-art facilities, experienced faculty members, and strong clinical collaborations, we strive to create a nurturing environment that fosters excellence in medical care, education, and research.

Technology Demonstrator Project

Rapidly Deployable Cardiac Digital Twin

The project is developing a cardiac digital twin (CDT) to improve precision in treating ventricular tachycardia (VT) and identify the risk of sudden cardiac death (SCD). The CDT will integrate MRI and ECG data to create patient-specific heart models that accurately replicate electrical activity and pathological conduction pathways. By simulating impulse propagation, the model will identify optimal ablation targets, minimizing damage to healthy tissue. Validated through retrospective and prospective studies, the CDT will also assess SCD risk by identifying arrhythmogenic patterns.

=== non invasive medical imaging diagnostics ===
Source: https://www.iitk.ac.in/gsmst/non-invasive-medical-imaging-diagnostics
CoE: Non-invasive Imaging and Diagnostics

The centre focuses on developing improved imaging technologies, novel measurement configurations, data interpretation algorithms for enhanced diagnostics, and specialised instrumentation. MedTech tools, including microscopy, endoscopy, ultrasound, MRI, and X-ray, are critical for correct diagnosis and follow-up treatment. While these tools are serving to the best of their capabilities, they have limitations with the quality of resolution, volume imaging, and ease of use for the healthcare provider and the patient. The CoE aims to address these problems by providing engineering solutions to overcome the limitations of the existing tools and innovating novel tools with better performance metrics.

CoE: Tropical and Infectious Disease

The CoE for infectious diseases focuses on technological advancement and the development of diagnostics, therapeutics, and preventive measures. The centre is establishing methods and preventive tools to restrain the spread of infectious diseases, early and accurate diagnosis, and prognosis of infectious diseases. Ongoing research is developing appropriate therapeutics to cure infectious diseases.

Technology Demonstrator Project

Technology Platform for Rapid and Total Eradication of Topical Gram-negative Bacteria via Plasmonic Heating

The project is developing a technology to eradicate gram-negative bacterial cells selectively and efficiently, without affecting host cells. The first step is a non-lethal approach, where bacterial cells will be kept intentionally alive so that the host cells stay safe. This will be done using functionalized plasmonic nanoparticles (PNPs), which will selectively attach to Gram-negative bacterial cells and crosslink them in clusters but will keep them alive. The second step involves a lethal approach, where those PNP-bound bacterial clusters will be laser irradiated to kill all bacterial cells, just like photothermal therapy kills cancer cells. Host cells will remain unaffected.
