=== research scholars day ===
Source: https://www.iitk.ac.in/msp/research-scholars-day
The research scholars' day (RS Day) is an annual event celebrated by the MTech and PhD students of the Materials Science Programme.As part of the programme, the students showcase their research work through posters and presentations to the attendees, which helps in recognition, feedback, collaborations, etc. The purpose of the research scholar day is to promote research awareness among the new postgraduate students. Usually, most of our faculty members attend it, which promotes a strong student-faculty interaction.

Research Scholars Day’ 2020
Research Scholars’ Day is an annual event of Materials Science Programme where all the students from M.Tech. and Ph.D. showcase their research work via posters/ presentation. It helps them with critical feed backs about their work, collaborations etc. The purpose of this day is to promote research awareness among the new postgraduate students. Many faculty members attend the programme, which promotes a strong student-faculty interaction.
