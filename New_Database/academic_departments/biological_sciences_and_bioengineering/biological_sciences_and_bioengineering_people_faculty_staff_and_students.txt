Institutional
Animal Ethics Committee (IAEC)

(CPCSEA Registration No.: 810/GO/ReBi/2003/CPCSEA)

Member
Secretary: Dr Rashmi Parihar,  [Email: iaec@
/ Tel: 2110]

Guidelines for
submitting application for ethical clearance by the IAEC

Step 1: Submit a hard copy of the application to the
office of the Member Secretary, IAEC. A soft copy of the application should
also be submitted via email.

The
application form can be downloaded from this link: <<IAEC
Protocol Form>>

Please
note that the IAEC normally meets twice in a year (September and March),
therefore the application should be submitted at least 30 days in advance
[Context: DEPARTMENT OF BIOLOGICAL SCIENCES AND BIOENGINEERING IIT KANPUR - FACULTY, STAFF, STUDENTS, ALU > committee iaec]
(i.e., by July 31 for the September meeting, and by January 31 for the March
meeting)

While
preparing the application please note the following:

–       For animal experiments requiring the use of
bio-hazardous substances, recombinant DNA/vaccine and/or samples from human
origin, the study protocol should be first cleared by the Institute Bio-Safety
Committee (IBSC) and/or the Institutional Ethics Committee for experimental
involving human subjects (IEC) as applicable.

–       Currently, the IAEC can only handle
applications for experiments involving rat or mouse (we do not have housing
facilities for other animals).

–       The protocol should clearly mention the source
of animals. CDRI, Lucknow is an approved source of these animals in the
[Context: DEPARTMENT OF BIOLOGICAL SCIENCES AND BIOENGINEERING IIT KANPUR - FACULTY, STAFF, STUDENTS, ALU > committee iaec]
neighbourhood.

The
application will be evaluated by the committee (and subject expert if required)
and the applicant may be asked to furnish other documents if required.

Step 2: A meeting of IAEC will be scheduled and the
date will be communicate to the applicant at least 20 days in advance. The
applicant will be asked to present his/her proposal to the committee and a
decision will be communicated to applicant within a week.

All applicants are advised to
go through the guidelines given by the Committee for the Purpose
of Control and Supervision of Experiments on Animals (CPCSEA).
