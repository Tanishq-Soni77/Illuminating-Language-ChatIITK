Prof. Neeraj Misra was bestowed the Distinguished Teacher Award during IIT Kanpur's annual Teacher's Day celebrations. Congratulations Neeraj!

Prof. Dootika Vats was one of the winners of the COPSS Emerging Leader awards for 2026 presented during the Joint Statistical Meeting held in Boston. Congratulations Dootika!

On 18th July, the Department was delighted to host a Conference on Statistical Theory and Applications in honour of Prof. Debasis Kundu who superannuated last month. It was a wonderful occasion to celebrate Prof. Kundu’s remarkable contributions to statistical theory and applications, and the lasting impact he has had on his students, collaborators, and the broader statistical community.
