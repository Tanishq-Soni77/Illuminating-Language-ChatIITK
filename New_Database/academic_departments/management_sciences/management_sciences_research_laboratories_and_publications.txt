Flyer of the book

2021
About the book:
This edited book addresses the challenges in managing the operations and supply chain of organizations in the era of the internet of things and Industry 4.0. It presents cutting-edge research on real-world operations-related problems, in-depth analyses, and relevant managerial implications. A wide variety of solution approaches such as quantitative, quantitative, and simulations are presented in the context of managing the operations and supply chains. This book caters to a wide audience in different disciplines related to business management, economics, and engineering due to the presentation of different solution methodologies.
[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MANAGEMENT SCIENCES (DOMS) IIT KANPUR - RESEARCH AREAS, LABORATORIES, FACILITIES, > books]
This book is an outcome of the XXIII Annual International Conference of Society of Operations Management organized by the Department of Industrial & Management Engineering, IIT Kanpur, and includes selected research papers from the conference in addition to the invited research papers.
