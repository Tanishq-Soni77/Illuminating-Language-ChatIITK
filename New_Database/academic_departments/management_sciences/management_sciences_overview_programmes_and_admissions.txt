=== about us ===
Source: https://www.iitk.ac.in/doms/about-us
Set up in 1974 as an interdisciplinary program, the Department of Management Sciences (DoMS) at IIT Kanpur was one of the first in the country to leverage the analytical education of engineers in the training of management professionals. The program subsequently evolved as a full-fledged department in 1988. Under the aegis of IIT Kanpur, known for its vibrant academic culture, the DoMS department has become one of the well regarded departments of industrial engineering and management in India.

The department currently has 28 faculty members with PhDs from reputed universities within and outside India, having interests in diverse fields such as quantitative and statistical decision modeling, operations and supply management, manufacturing systems, finance, marketing, economics, analytics, infrastructure, IT, Innovation, Intellectual Property Management and knowledge management. In keeping with the overall academic ethos of IIT Kanpur, teaching and research are pursued with passion and dedication. Faculty members are also engaged in a diverse set of activities including industry consultancy, management development programs and public sector projects. Some senior faculty members from this department have gone on to leadership roles in several Indian universities, or gained other recognitions: Director of NITIE, Director of IIM Lucknow, Director of IIIT Bangalore, Vice Chancellor of UPTU, and President of the Operations Research Society of India.

The department encourages active collaboration with industry as well as other academic institutions. The department receives several faculty applications every year from candidates interested in its research oriented environment. The aim of the department is to continue to excel in its research and training programs, promoting both technical and managerial skills as well as higher ethics and values.

The curriculum at the department comprises state-of-the-art techniques to plan, design, implement and manage integrated systems of people, material, capital, information and technology. The subjects taught provide a well-rounded coverage of the methods to manage and improve productivity, customer satisfaction and profitability of industrial, information and infrastructural (service) systems. In today's competitive era these methods have rapidly become relevant for engineers in all disciplines. Consequently, fresh engineering graduates as well as practicing professionals in fields ranging from computer science to aerospace engineering and architecture to electronics engineering regularly enroll in DoMS's postgraduate programs.

The department currently offers Ph.D., M.Tech. and M.B.A. programs in Department of Management Sciences. The Ph.D. program is widely regarded as one of the best programs in management research in India. Candidates with master's degrees from diverse academic backgrounds go through a rigorous selection process to gain admission to the Ph.D. program. Research scholars undergo courses in management disciplines such as Quantitative and Statistical Decision Modeling, Operations and Supply Management, Manufacturing Systems, Finance, Marketing, Organizational Behavior, Innovation, Intellectual Property Management, Quality Models, Service Science & Management, E-Governance etc. Doctoral students from DoMS have found faculty positions at reputed universities such as University of Florida, IIT Delhi, IIT Kharagpur, IIIT Jabalpur and IIM Calcutta.

[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MANAGEMENT SCIENCES (DOMS) IIT KANPUR - OVERVIEW, HISTORY, PROGRAMMES, ADMISSIONS > about us]
The M.Tech. program is the earliest program offered by the department. It provides a perfect blend of coursework in industrial engineering and management sciences. Admission is selective, a high GATE score being a prerequisite. Students take a year of course work followed by a year-long dissertation on a thesis topic. The intervening summer internships are used for practical project work in industries. Several alumni of the M.Tech. program have done our department proud in both the corporate world and academia with careers in reputed companies such as HSBC Analytics, Citibank, Symphony, GENPACT, IBM, Oracle, Tata Motors, Tata Steel, Reliance, Texas A&M University and IIMs.

The MBA program was started in 2001, keeping in mind the growing need for managers with a strong technical background. Admission is through the Common Admission Test (CAT) followed by personal interview and group discussion. The syllabus includes several core courses in the first year that provide a broad foundation in management and include a strong practical component, with industrial projects incorporated in the courses. In the second year, students have the flexibility to choose among several electives depending on their individual interests and career goals. Conscious effort is made to keep the course content relevant to the Indian scenario. The case study method is used extensively to encourage participatory learning. The program has a mandatory two month summer internship component. Specialization offered in MBA includes sectorial areas of Service and Manufacturing and functional areas of Operations, Systems, Marketing, and Finance. Recent graduates of the MBA program have been placed in reputed Indian and multinational companies such as GENPACT, Accenture, ICICI Bank, ABN AMRO, Ashok Leyland, ACC, Citibank, Honda, LG, Maruti Udyog, Siemens, and TCS.

In order to be able to offer state-of-the-art academic programs, the department seeks to collaborate with reputed Asian, European and US universities. This will enable the department to globalize its academic research and teaching paradigm. At present the department has collaborations with RPI, USA, and METEC Defence University College (Ethiopia).

Our Vision

To excel in both industrial and management education and research to have a long lasting impact on management theory and practices in academia as well as in industry.

Core Competence

Aside from teaching, the faculty are engaged in wide research areas which include Operations Research, Operations Management, Decision Support Systems, Flexible and Computer Integrated Manufacturing Systems, Industrial Engineering, Organization Sciences, Personnel Management and Industrial Relations, Small Business Management, Total Quality Management, Manufacturing Strategy, Management of Technology , Strategic Management, Knowledge Management, Marketing and Consumer Behavior, e-Commerce, Entrepreneurship and Technological Innovation, Infrastructure Finance and Policy, Intellectual Property Rights.

Facilities

The department is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities for computational support. The System Optimization laboratory provides computational support both for teaching and for research. It includes connected Pentiums and Workstations to form a local area network, and provide access to the computing facilities of the institute and Internet.

To support its various academic programs the department already has a separate dedicated academic building, and it would strive and maintain the full utilization of its 10 class rooms/seminar rooms/MDP class rooms, library, faculty lounge, 30 faculty rooms, rooms for PhD. students and computer labs.

Greetings from the Department of Management Sciences!!

The Department of Management Sciences (DoMS) (erstwhile Department of Industrial and Management Engineering), IIT Kanpur, is first of its kind in the country to leverage the analytical education of engineers to train them into management professionals. Established as an interdisciplinary program in 1974, it subsequently evolved as a full-fledged department in 1988 and given the diversity of its programs from MBA, MTech, PhD, eMasters etc. has metamorphosed into the Department of Management Sciences (DoMS) from Department of Industrial and Management Engineering (DIME).

The department presently comprises a substantial number of faculty members with doctorates & post doctorate qualification from top institutions within and outside India, having interests in diverse fields. The department places strong emphasis on high-impact research published in leading journals in the areas such as operations research, operations management, decision support systems, organization sciences, finance, marketing, economics, analytics, entrepreneurship, energy etc.

ABOUT

The 2-year MBA program at IIT Kanpur is designed to nurture business leaders and entrepreneurs by leveraging the institute’s strengths in technology, management and social sciences. The curriculum provides a balanced mix of domain knowledge across Analytics, Marketing, Finance, HR, Operations and IT/Consulting.

Capstone project:

At IIT Kanpur, we believe true learning happens through practice - nearly 70% comes from hands-on experience. The Capstone Project embodies this philosophy, enabling students to gain real-world problem-solving skills and business insights in a technology-driven environment. Developed in collaboration with industry experts, faculty, and alumni, the project allows students to address business challenges, design innovative products and services, and deliver measurable value.

[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MANAGEMENT SCIENCES (DOMS) IIT KANPUR - OVERVIEW, HISTORY, PROGRAMMES, ADMISSIONS > capstone]
Key objectives:

Foster innovation and sustainability while preparing students for future demands.

Apply cross-functional knowledge to practical business problems.

Provide companies with actionable insights and impactful solutions.

The Capstone runs parallel to coursework and can be pursued as a short-or long-term engagement- either as an extension of internships or as independent live projects. By bridging academic learning with practical application, it equips students to create meaningful, industry-relevant outcomes.

About the Conference

ICFMCF is a prestigious pan-IIT initiative, jointly organized by the leading management schools of the Indian Institute of Technology (IITs), including IIT Madras, IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Dhanbad, IIT Roorkee, IIT Kanpur, and IIT Kharagpur. ICFMCF is an annual event of GAFER (Global Association of Financial and Entrepreneurial Research) which has been established by finance group of all IITs. The PAN-IIT conference brings together researchers from academia, industry, and regulatory organizations to deliberate on theoretical work, empirical findings and policy implications related to financial markets and corporate finance. The 8th Pan-IIT INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE ON FINANCIAL MARKETS & CORPORATE FINANCE (ICFMCF 2026) will be held in the Department of Management Studies, Indian Institute of Technology Madras, from July 3 - 5, 2026. Since its inception, ICFMCF has grown into one of the most respected events in the discipline, known for its rigorous review standards, high-quality paper presentations, keynote lectures by eminent scholars, and opportunities for global networking and academic collaboration.

[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MANAGEMENT SCIENCES (DOMS) IIT KANPUR - OVERVIEW, HISTORY, PROGRAMMES, ADMISSIONS > icfmcf]
The aim of the conference is to provide a forum to showcase the cutting-edge research in the areas of financial markets, corporate finance & accounting, and sustainable finance. The conference provides an excellent avenue to get quality feedback and an opportunity for networking among researchers, academicians, and industry executives working in the area of finance and allied areas. We invite high-quality original theoretical/applied empirical papers from faculty members, research scholars, and industry practitioners. The conference encourages interdisciplinary research and fosters knowledge sharing that contributes to both academia and industry.

About Global Association of Financial and Entrepreneurial Research (GAFER)

[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MANAGEMENT SCIENCES (DOMS) IIT KANPUR - OVERVIEW, HISTORY, PROGRAMMES, ADMISSIONS > icfmcf]
The Global Association of Financial and Entrepreneurial Research (GAFER) is a non-profit society established to foster high-impact research, meaningful academic collaborations, and forward-looking policy dialogue in the fields of finance, economics, and entrepreneurship. GAFER’s flagship event is the ICFMCF, organized annually in collaboration Indian Institute of Technology (IITs), including IIT Madras, IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Dhanbad, IIT Roorkee, IIT Kanpur, and IIT Kharagpur.  Through its various initiatives, GAFER plays a pivotal role in shaping the research landscape and nurturing a global community of scholars committed to excellence.

Frequently Asked Questions

When and where will the conference be held?

The 8th Pan-IIT International Conference on Financial Markets & Corporate Finance (ICFMCF 2026) will be held from 3–5 July 2026 at the Department of Management Studies, IIT Madras, Chennai, India.

[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MANAGEMENT SCIENCES (DOMS) IIT KANPUR - OVERVIEW, HISTORY, PROGRAMMES, ADMISSIONS > icfmcf faqs]
What are the major tracks of the conference?

The conference invites research papers under the following broad tracks:

Financial Markets

Corporate Finance & Accounting

Sustainable Finance

AI in Finance

What type of submissions are accepted?

Only full papers are accepted for submission. Abstracts or extended abstracts are not accepted.

What is the last date for paper submission?

The last date for submission of the full paper is 31 March 2026. Early submission is encouraged.

When will the acceptance notification be communicated?

Authors will receive the notification of acceptance on 20 April 2026.

How should the paper be formatted?

Papers should follow these formatting guidelines:

Font: Times New Roman

[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MANAGEMENT SCIENCES (DOMS) IIT KANPUR - OVERVIEW, HISTORY, PROGRAMMES, ADMISSIONS > icfmcf faqs]
Font size: 12-point

Line spacing: 1.5

References: Single spacing

Citation style: APA style

Submission format: PDF only

Each page should be numbered and tables/figures should be properly labeled.

Is there any word limit or page limit for paper submission?

There is no specific word or page limit prescribed for the paper submission.

How can I submit my paper?

Authors must submit their papers online through the conference submission portal. Authors must first create login credentials before uploading their paper.

What are the registration fees?

Registration fees (excluding GST) are approximately:

Research Scholars: INR 4000 (Early) / INR 5000 (Regular)

Faculty Members: INR 6000 (Early) / INR 8000 (Regular)

[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MANAGEMENT SCIENCES (DOMS) IIT KANPUR - OVERVIEW, HISTORY, PROGRAMMES, ADMISSIONS > icfmcf faqs]
Industry Participants: INR 10,000 (Early) / INR 12,000 (Regular)

International Participants: USD 200 (Early) / USD 250 (Regular)

Do all authors need to register?

At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present the paper at the conference. If more than one author wants to attend the Conference, they also have to register individually.

Will certificates be provided?

Yes. Certificates will include the names of all authors of the accepted paper.

Who should attend the conference?

The conference is intended for:

Faculty members and researchers

PhD and research scholars

Master’s Students

Finance and fintech professionals

Industry practitioners

Policymakers and regulators

Is there an option for online or virtual presentation?

[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MANAGEMENT SCIENCES (DOMS) IIT KANPUR - OVERVIEW, HISTORY, PROGRAMMES, ADMISSIONS > icfmcf faqs]
No. Online or virtual presentation is not available. All accepted papers must be presented physically at the conference venue.

How many papers can a person submit?

A person can submit more than one paper but can present only one paper. If there are co-authors, the co-author can register and present the next paper.

When should the registration fee be paid?

The registration fee must be paid after the paper has been accepted.

Will conference proceedings be published?

The conference proceedings are yet to be finalized. Once finalized, the details will be updated on the conference website.

Can undergraduate students submit papers?

Yes. Undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral students are welcome to submit papers.

[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MANAGEMENT SCIENCES (DOMS) IIT KANPUR - OVERVIEW, HISTORY, PROGRAMMES, ADMISSIONS > icfmcf faqs]
Can a paper have multiple authors?

Yes. Papers may have multiple authors, but at least one author must register and present the paper.

Who should I contact for further queries?

For any additional queries, participants may contact the Conference Secretariat through the contact details provided on the conference website.

Will accepted papers be published in Scopus-indexed proceedings?

After the conference, authors will have the opportunity to submit their papers for consideration for publication in Scopus-indexed conference proceedings.

How to reach IIT Madras

Reaching IIT Campus on arrival to Chennai

Location of IIT Madras

The IIT Madras campus is located on the Sardar Patel Road, half-way between the Raj Bhavan and Adayar bus terminus, i.e. about 12 kms from Chennai Central. The other landmarks are the Guindy Snake Park adjacent to the IIT Main gate, Gandhi Mandapam (Anna University Stop) and the Central Leather Research Institute (CLRI) campus Opposite to IIT Madras. It is also near to the Rajbhavan which is the residence of His Excellency the Governor of Tamil Nadu. The following are the ways to reach IIT Madras from railway stations (Central & Egmore), Airport, and Chennai Mofussil Bus Terminus (CMBT):

[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MANAGEMENT SCIENCES (DOMS) IIT KANPUR - OVERVIEW, HISTORY, PROGRAMMES, ADMISSIONS > icfmcf how to reach iit madras]
From Chennai Central

If you are traveling with light luggage, you can take a city bus to IIT. The bus stop is opposite to the Central Station .The direct buses to the campus are 19B and 5C. Alternatively you can reach Saidapet by taking a Bus bearing No.18 and from there number of buses goes to Adayar and they are 5B, 47, 47A, 47D, 147, 23C etc. You can get down in front of the IIT Main Gate. Or else you can take a bus to Adayar Signal i.e. the bus bearing these numbers 1LSS, 4LSS, 1A,1C,1D,6A will go in that direction. From Adyar you can travel in either of these buses 5B, 47, 47A, 47D, 147, 23C to reach IIT Madras Main Gate.

Local Train

You can walk down to Park Railway Station which is opposite to the Central Station and take an EMU (local) train to reach Kasturba Nagar (Adayar) or Guindy or Saidapet station and reach the campus from there by the buses mentioned above. It is advisable that do not use this mode if you are traveling with a heavy luggage.

[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MANAGEMENT SCIENCES (DOMS) IIT KANPUR - OVERVIEW, HISTORY, PROGRAMMES, ADMISSIONS > icfmcf how to reach iit madras]
Taxi or Auto

If you are carrying a heavy luggage (you are suppose to be) it would be convenient for you to hire an auto or a taxi. Chennai Central has a prepaid auto/taxi service and this is manned by the City Police. The fare to the campus from Chennai Central will be around Rs.150/- for an auto and Rs.250/- for a taxi. When you travel either by Auto or Taxe you can get dropped at the Taramani Guest House, which is about 3.2 kms from the IIT Main Gate.

Chennai-Egmore

If you arrive at the Chennai Egmore station, you can take 23C to IIT or a 23E, 23A, 23G to Adayar Signal from where there are a number of buses to IIT. Current auto and taxi fares from Egmore are around Rs.120/- and Rs.225/- respectively. You can also make use of call taxi service mentioned below. Alternatively, a local train to Guindy can also be taken.

[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MANAGEMENT SCIENCES (DOMS) IIT KANPUR - OVERVIEW, HISTORY, PROGRAMMES, ADMISSIONS > icfmcf how to reach iit madras]
From Airport

If you arrive at the Chennai Meenambakkam Airport, you can make your way to the Institute in an auto or a taxi or a Call-Taxi.

Alternatively, if you want to come by city bus, you can take PP21 to reach the Institute or 21G to IIT (bus stop) which is very near from the main gate, or you can find lot of buses to Saidapet from the airport. From Saidapet you get many buses to the Institute which are mentioned above.

From Bus Terminus

If you are traveling by bus all along from your town and reach Chennai Mofussil Bus Terminus, commonly known as CMBT, from CMBT, you can come to institute by taking city bus 23M or by Call-Taxi or Auto. The fares would be Rs.150/- by auto and Rs.250/- by car.

[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MANAGEMENT SCIENCES (DOMS) IIT KANPUR - OVERVIEW, HISTORY, PROGRAMMES, ADMISSIONS > icfmcf how to reach iit madras]
From Main Gate to Taramani Guest House (TGH)

Those who are coming to campus by city buses can use internal transport to reach TGH. IIT runs buses from the main gate to the hostel zone. These buses are run at a frequency of 15 minutes from 6:15 am till 10.00 pm.

=== m tech program overview ===
Source: https://www.iitk.ac.in/doms/m-tech-program-overview
M. Tech. Overview

Technological advances brought significant changes to organizations and industries across the globe, creating an ever-increasing demand for professionals with analytical and managerial skills combined with sound technical knowledge. Foreseeing this need,DoMS of IIT Kanpur designed its M.Tech Programme to gain and hone necessary managerial skills for engineers.

A high score in the GATE examination is the first step towards securing an admission in the program. The high scorers then appear in the Department's own tests on comprehension and quantitative skills followed by a personal interview are the only way to get admitted in the curriculum. Passing through a selection process that guarantee the best of the available talents, the students are challenged with an equally tough academic program that focus on both learning and implementation.

[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MANAGEMENT SCIENCES (DOMS) IIT KANPUR - OVERVIEW, HISTORY, PROGRAMMES, ADMISSIONS > m tech program overview]
The M.Tech program is the flagship program of DoMS. It is one of the oldest to introduce industrial engineering in India. The program provides a perfect blend of industrial engineering and management science to its students. The well-defined structure of course work and a yearlong thesis work provides in-depth knowledge of concepts to students. The intervening summer internships is used for practical project work in industries.

The program focuses on developing expertise in management engineering. It covers diverse topics from both the management and engineering paradigms. Quantitative modeling methods, improvement of productivity, quality and profitability in the manufacturing sector, supply chain management and application of optimization techniques in finance, marketing and IT are some of the areas to which students are exposed.

[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MANAGEMENT SCIENCES (DOMS) IIT KANPUR - OVERVIEW, HISTORY, PROGRAMMES, ADMISSIONS > m tech program overview]
DoMS emphasizes on the "methods" of understanding any systems. Rather than 'spoon-feeding', students are taught basic concepts and their analytical skills are then sharpened through a series of rigorous exercises. This approach helps to build strong reasoning and analytical abilities and best prepares them to take on real-world challenges.DoMS recognizes that mere course-work will not normally make one an effective manager. It is the rigor to which the DoMS faculty subjects its students, which imparts in them the potential to be a top notch manager.

In addition the department is equipped with the latest tools and systems used in the industries for the students to obtain hands-on experience. Having some of the most complicated software like product life cycle management, simulation, statistical analysis, financial modelling, process design and control, etc. in the well-equipped laboratories allow the students to learn the vital skills allowing them to succeed in future; both in industry and academia.

One-person-one-job-policy: A student once placed with a company shall not be allowed to participate in the placement process further. A dream company may also be given an advance slot/day during the placement week.

Placement Committee shall contact the organizations where the students of the graduating batch of 2013 have undergone the summer internships. Once a student accepts a PPO he/she will not be allowed to participate in the campus placement process, except in the case of a Dream Company.

Guidelines for admission to post-graduate programmes:

The department follows semester system. The first semester normally commences in the last week of July every year and the second in the last week of December. Admissions to the Ph.D. programme may be made in either or both of the two semesters. The Head of the Department may take the decision in this regard. Admissions to the Ph.D. programme are normally made in April-May for the first semester and in November-December for the second semester. The Dean of Academic Affairs will notify the admission calendar each semester. In addition, the department may process applications for admissions to Ph.D. Programmes on a continuous basis and admit students as per the existing procedure. The candidates admitted during the course of a semester, would be required to register for thesis with proportionate reduction in units.

Guidelines for admission to part-time post-graduate programmes:

The Institute also offers part-time postgraduate programmes leading to the M.Tech./MS (Research)/Ph.D. degrees for professionally employed personnel such as working engineers, scientists and teachers who can, while employed, attend regular classes as per schedule of the Institute. These students will have to give an undertaking endorsed by their employers that they will be attending all classes.

The applicant must be an employee of a recognized organization with at least two years of service at the time of admission and be engaged in professional work in the area to which admission is sought. An application for the part-time studies forwarded by the employer should be sent to the Convener, DPGC of the department concerned.

Admission to Ph.D. Programme, DoMS, IIT Kanpur

(Not applicable to NRI/PIO and foreign applicants)

The following stages are involved in the admissions process for the Ph.D. program of DoMS, IIT Kanpur.

STAGE 1:

Eligibility Criteria for Ph.D. in Management

The applicant must have a master's degree in Management or a master's degree in Engineering with marks / CPI not below the specified minimum*.

Those applicants may also be considered who have a bachelor's degree in Engineering or a 4-year bachelor's degree in Science with a minimum of 75 percent marks/7.5 CPI or a master's degree in Science/Arts/ Commerce while satisfying each of the following criteria: (a) a minimum of 65 percent marks/6.5 CPI in the master's degree; (b) first division in bachelor's degree; and (c) JRF/NET/95 percentile or higher in CAT or GMAT/GATE AIR within the top 5% of the number of candidates that appeared for the relevant paper, or qualifying in any other national level examination (if the master's degree is not from IITs/IISERs/NITs with a minimum CPI of 8.0).

*The "specified minimum" marks/CPI (Cumulative Performance Index), implies a minimum of 55 percent marks /5.5 (on a 10 point scale) as long as it is not less than the minimum pass marks/CPI; otherwise, the "specified minimum" marks/CPI implies the minimum pass marks/CPI.

STAGE 2:

Shortlisting of the applicants

The short-listing of applicants for the written test will be based on academic record as well as work experience and the total number of approved seats as well as the number of applicants in each category. Only eligible and completed applications will be considered for short-listing. A committee in-charge of Ph.D. admission will prepare the shortlists. A separate short list for each category will be prepared. Meeting the eligibility criteria does not guarantee short-listing for the written test. Only suitable applicants (to be judged by the Ph.D. admission committee) will be called for the written test.

Written test and Personal Interview

STAGE 4:

Selection List Preparation

A merit list will be prepared based on the applicant’s performance in the written test and the personal interview. Aggregation of marks for the eligible applicants (those who were interviewed) will be as per the weights given below.

Performance in Written test    40%

NOTE:

About PMRF

To improve the quality of research in various higher educational Indian institutions, Government of India introduced the Prime Minister's Research Fellows (PMRF) Scheme in 2018 for carrying out research in cutting edge science and technology domains, with focus on national priorities.
