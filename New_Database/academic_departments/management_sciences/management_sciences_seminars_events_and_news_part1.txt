M. Tech. Admission

M.Tech. Admissions 2026

All Indian national candidates (both GATE and Non-GATE) must apply through the admission portal available at the IIT Kanpur website https://iitk.ac.in/doaa/pgadmission/index.php. Application deadline: April 13, 2026.

Applicants must possess a B.E./B.Tech. degree in any discipline with a minimum CPI of 5.5/10 or 55% marks. Please refer to the PG manual posted on the DoAA website for detailed eligibility criteria.

Department of Management Sciences (DoMS) accepts GATE scores from any stream for admission to the M.Tech. program. GATE scores are valid for 3 years. Candidates who took GATE in 2024, 2025, or 2026 are eligible to apply.

[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MANAGEMENT SCIENCES (DOMS) IIT KANPUR - SEMINARS, EVENTS, WORKSHOPS, NEWS AND ANN > m tech announcement]
The GATE score requirement is waived for B.Tech. graduates from IITs with a minimum overall CPI of 6.5 and at least 8.0 CPI in the last two years of the B.Tech. program.

All GATE-based applicants must accept or reject the admission offer through the Common Offer Acceptance Portal (COAP) (https://coap2026.iitr.ac.in/coap2026/).
