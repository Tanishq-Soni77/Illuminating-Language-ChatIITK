PG Programmes in Sustainable Energy Engineering (SEE)

The academic programme of the Department of Sustainable Energy Engineering aims at a paradigm shift in education; by imparting sector-based knowledge, wherein concepts of several core disciplines are blended to provide an appropriate skill-set to its students.

The Department offers following academic programmes:

Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.)

Master of Technology (M.Tech.)

Master of Science by Research M.S.(R)

B.Tech. - M.Tech. Dual degree (for B.Tech students in other departments)

Information for Prospective Students

AI for Sustainability

Overview:

This interdisciplinary programme equips students with technical, ethical, and AI-driven expertise to design innovative solutions for global sustainability challenges. With hands-on learning and a global outlook, students will be prepared for impactful careers in industry, government, international organisations, startups, and corporate ESG groups. Master’s projects will also strengthen KSS’s research ecosystem.

[Context: KOTAK SCHOOL OF SUSTAINABILITY IIT KANPUR - COURSES, CURRICULA AND TIMETABLES > pg course]
Career Pathways:

Graduates can pursue roles as senior executives, policy makers, urban planners, engineers, architects, real estate professionals, forecasters, sustainability managers, social scientists, and data scientists.

Eligibility:

B.Tech. in Civil, Mechanical, Chemical, Environmental, Sustainable Energy, Electrical & Electronics, Computer Science & Engineering, Data Science; M.Sc. in Physics, Maths & Statistics, Atmospheric/Climate Sciences; or Economic Sciences
