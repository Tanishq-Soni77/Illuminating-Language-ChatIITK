Head

Message from Head of the Department

Materials Science and Engineering (MSE) Department at IIT Kanpur is home to world-renown faculty
and caters to 300+ B.Tech., 50+ M.Tech., and 125+ Ph.D. scholars. Research-groups in the MSE
department have made global impact in the domains from conventional metallurgy and iron- and
steel-making to semiconducting/electronic materials, biomaterials, advanced material processing,
coatings/corrosion protection, automotive/aerospace, batteries, packaging/sensors, defense
applications, etc. Correlation among material selection, synthesis and processing, characterization,
and performance has been made possible via in-house capability of high-end research equipment,
[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MATERIALS SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING IIT KANPUR - FACULTY, STAFF, STUDENTS, ALUMNI,  > head]
accelerated material design and testing, high-throughput experiments, in-situ evaluation,
computational-modeling, material-data software/ visualization, etc.
MSE Department welcomes students/faculty/staff who wish to challenge themselves and explore
multidisciplinary science and engineering paradigm of metallurgy and materials. Industries/firms are
invited to share bottlenecks and share those technical problems via research
projects/consultancy/CSR, and we will be happy to work-together and provide probable solutions.
MSE alumni are requested to stay connected, provide feedback/give advice on critical issues, and
contribute positively to the growth of the department. Let us contribute to nation building, and
[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MATERIALS SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING IIT KANPUR - FACULTY, STAFF, STUDENTS, ALUMNI,  > head]
march towards creating a new world...together.

Upskill your oratory skills and  overcome your stage fright! MSE-RSD23 provides you a platform to present your work among departmental colleagues for  constructive feedback. Join us to  cheer your peers for various events!

Oral Presentation

The total time allotted to each speaker is 12 mins. You should plan to speak for 10 mins and 2 mins for questions.

The talk will be judged based on presentation skills, slides design and
questionnaire session.

Slides must be clear and must convey information.

Slides must not be crowded/ clustered and must not contain low resolution
[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MATERIALS SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING IIT KANPUR - FACULTY, STAFF, STUDENTS, ALUMNI,  > rsd 2023 guidelines]
images.

References may be put at the end or in slide itself as per requirement.

There will be best oral presentation awards given separately for each of the
different sessions based on their fields of research.

Poster Presentation

Poster Can be designed using Microsoft Power Point or Photoshop.

Authors are requested to use good quality paper and printing of their
choice.

Poster should contain, "Title, details of authors and their affiliation,
introduction, experimental, results and discussions, conclusions and
references wherever applicable".

Please ensure that the front size of the text in poster is readable. Contents
should be readable from at least 3 ft distance.

Colour schemes can be used as desired and should aid readability of
[Context: DEPARTMENT OF MATERIALS SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING IIT KANPUR - FACULTY, STAFF, STUDENTS, ALUMNI,  > rsd 2023 guidelines]
contents.

Multiple columns can be used if required.

Leave ample border around the poster for ease of sticking on the display
board.

There will be best poster awards given separately for each of the different
field of research.
