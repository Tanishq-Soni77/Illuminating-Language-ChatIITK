=== optical and infrared astronomy laboratory ===
Source: https://www.iitk.ac.in/space/optical-and-infrared-astronomy-laboratory
Experiment modules consist of experiments related to electromagnetic wave detection and analyses in X-ray/optical/IR/radio bands, observing fundamental principles (interference, diffraction, coherence etc.), setting up spectroscopy and polarimetry experiments, and carrying out related measurements applicable in the field of astronomy, planetary sciences.

We are developing an Adaptive Optics (AO) test bench based on the Shack–Hartmann wavefront sensor, which will be used to train students on AO and wavefront control techniques.

=== planetary science laboratory ===
Source: https://www.iitk.ac.in/space/planetary-science-laboratory
The Planetary Science laboratory aims to demonstrate and uncover through experiments geophysical processes active in and responsible for the features observed on planetary objects and their dynamical evolution. For this, presently we are working with rotating ionised fluids in a magnetic field, flowing grains with interspersed boulders, and waves through heterogeneous solids. The aim is to investigate the structure of rings within Saturn's magnetosphere, the reshaping of granular minor planets like the asteroids Ryugu and Bennu due to landslides, and the impact-induced texturing of small bodies like the asteroid Itokawa.
