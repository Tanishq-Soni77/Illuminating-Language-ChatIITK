Course LT601 - INTRODUCTION TO LASERS
Department: Department of LT. Credits (L-T-P-D-C): 3-0-0--4. Source: Courses of Study 2021, IIT Kanpur.
Introduction to general lasers and their types, Brief intro. quantum physics, Schrodinger wave eqn., Atomic systems, emission and absorption processes, Population inversion, gain, optical cavities, three and four level lasers, CW and pulsed lasers, Q switching and mode locking, Physics of gas discharge, Atomic, Ionic, molecular, liquid, and excimer lasers, Optical pumping.

Course LT611 - LASER SYSTEMS AND APPLICATIONS
Department: Department of LT. Credits (L-T-P-D-C): 3-0-0--4. Source: Courses of Study 2021, IIT Kanpur.
Atomic, ionic, molecular, excimer and liquid laser systems and applications, Solid state lasers. Short Pulse generation and measurement. Laser applications in medicine and surgery, Materials processing, Optical Communication Lasers, Metrology and LIDAR.

Course LT631 - INTRODUCTION TO COHERENT AND LASER OPTICS
Department: Department of LT. Credits (L-T-P-D-C): 3-0-3--4. Source: Courses of Study 2021, IIT Kanpur.
Maxwell’s eqns. and electromagnetic waves, Wave properties of light and propagation. Waveguides, Optical resonators. Ray Optics, Matrix methods, Optical Anisotropic materials. Interference and interferometers, Diffraction and Fourier Optics, Optical components (Laser mirrors, windows, Polarizors, Holography, Meteorology).
