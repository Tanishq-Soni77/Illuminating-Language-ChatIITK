Name: ISRO-IIT Kanpur Space Technology Cell (STC IITK), website https://www.iitk.ac.in/stc/
Type: A joint research programme/cell of ISRO and IIT Kanpur under the Dean of Research and Development (DORD), established on 2 March 2001 by a Memorandum of Understanding between the Chairman of ISRO and the Director of IIT Kanpur. It funds and coordinates space-related research projects at IIT Kanpur. THIS IS NOT the Students' Gymkhana "Science and Technology Council" (SnT) and not a student club. Coordinator/In-charge: Prof. Bishakh Bhattacharya (Department of Mechanical Engineering). Joint Programme Committee (JPC) chaired by the Director of IIT Kanpur (Dr. Manindra Agrawal) with an ISRO co-chair (Dr. Ramanagouda V Nadagouda, URSC). The site shows Dr. V. Narayanan as Chairman, ISRO. Contact: stc@iitk.ac.in.

About Us

Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur carries out original research of significance and technology development at the cutting edge. It imparts training for students to make them competent, motivated engineers and scientists. The Institute not only celebrates freedom of thought, cultivates vision and encourages growth, but also inculcates human values and concern for the environment and the society.

IITK

The ISRO-IITK Space Technology Cell was established on March 02, 2001 with the signing of Memorandum of Understanding between the Chairman, Indian Space Research Organization and the Director, Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur with the realization of growing importance attached to the extending scope for generation of knowledge through advanced academic Research & Development efforts to ensure a truly self-reliant and self generating Space Programmes for our nation in future years.Read More

Our Vision
	"To march ahead as the torchbearers of the scientific community by initiating Space Programmes of world class importance, putting India in the reckoning as a potential global player with the state-of-art technology"

Mission
	To visualize and identify the challenging issues & the complex multi-disciplinary problems in the years to come by adopting a proactive approach regarding the future needs of the Indian Space Programme through extensive research base and optimal utilization of knowledge, expertise and experience.

Objective
	We, at Space Technology Cell, intend to harness the advanced areas of space science & technology by taking up quality and quantity initiatives to carry out researches of high importance to the Indian Space Programme, thereby contribute to the all round development of nation.

[Context: ISRO-IITK SPACE TECHNOLOGY CELL (STC), IIT KANPUR > STC page: /stc/wepledge.htm]
Strategy
	The collaborative efforts of IIT Kanpur with the Indian Space Research Organization and various other Space Centres have been extremely helpful in bringing us closer to achieving our objectives.

Focal Points
	1) Maximize research base, expertise and experience.
2) Generate basic knowledge through advanced academic researches.
3) Tackle multi-disciplinary problems in the Space Technology.
4) Focus specially on the ongoing Space Programmes and the application areas.

Thrust Areas
	1) Structural Engineering dealing with Composites and Smart Structures.
2) Dynamic Analysis and Control of Spacecraft and Launch Vehicles.
3) Software related to different applications.
4) Identification of new Advanced SpaceTechnology and its Application Areas.

2025
 IITK Experts

IIT Kanpur's Space Technology Cell (STC) successfully organized the "25th Annual Joint Policy Committee (JPC) meeting" on February 5, 2025, at the Outreach Centre, IIT Kanpur. The meeting was conducted in a hybrid mode, with participation both online and offline.

Dr. Brij Bhushan, Deputy Director chaired the meeting on behalf of the Director, IIT Kanpur, while Shri Sumitesh Sarkar served as the Co-Chairman, ISRO. Senior faculty members from IIT Kanpur — Dr. Tarun Gupta, Dean of Research and Development (DoRD); Dr. Bishakh Bhattacharya; Dr. G. M. Kamath; Dr. Yogesh Singh Chauhan; and Dr. Kumar Vaibhav Srivastava — attended the meeting in person.

[Context: ISRO-IITK SPACE TECHNOLOGY CELL (STC), IIT KANPUR > STC page: /stc/interactions.htm]
Representing ISRO, Shri R. V. Nadagouda (URSC), Dr. J. Krishna Kishore (URSC, Bengaluru), and Smt. Nirupama Tiwari (ISRO HQ, Bengaluru) were present physically. Additionally, numerous scientists from various ISRO centers joined the proceedings online, contributing actively to the discussions.

The meeting witnessed productive deliberations aimed at strengthening collaborative research initiatives between IIT Kanpur and ISRO under the Space Technology Cell framework.

2024
 IITK Experts

The 24th Annual Joint Policy Committee (JPC) meeting of the Space Technology Cell (STC), IIT Kanpur, was held on March 12, 2024, in hybrid mode. Chaired by Dr. S. Ganesh, Officiating Director, IIT Kanpur, and co-chaired by Shri Sumitesh Sarkar from ISRO, the meeting highlighted the launch of IITK’s new Department of Space, Planetary & Astronomical Sciences & Engineering (SPASE) and the strengthened collaboration with ISRO. Senior ISRO officials emphasized timely project completion, achieving higher Technology Readiness Levels (TRLs), and enhancing skill development. Discussions also focused on promoting new technologies, especially in Space Robotics, and leveraging industry-academia partnerships. The meeting reflected a strong commitment to advancing space research and innovation through collaborative efforts.

[Context: ISRO-IITK SPACE TECHNOLOGY CELL (STC), IIT KANPUR > STC page: /stc/interactions.htm]
Dr. Tarun Gupta, Dean of Research and Development (DoRD); Dr. Bishakh Bhattacharya; Dr. G. M. Kamath; Dr. Kallol Mondal; and Dr. Kumar Vaibhav Srivastava — attended the meeting in person.

Representing ISRO, Shri N Sudheer Kumar, Director, CBPO, ISRO, HQ, Dr. V. Ashok, (VSSC), Shri R. V. Nadagouda (URSC), Shri Muthuchezhian S(SHAR), Shri Raghavendra M R(ISTRAC) and Dr. M. A. Paul (ISRO HQ, Bengaluru) were present physically.

The principal investigators of both ongoing and newly submitted proposals presented their project updates during the meeting. Scientists from various ISRO centers also joined the session virtually and actively contributed to the discussions, further enriching the collaborative efforts between ISRO and IIT Kanpur.

 IITK Experts

IIT Kanpur celebrated National Technology Day 2019 with a thematic program titled "Space Technologies and ISRO" organized by Space Technology Cell, IITK. A couple of popular talks were delivered by scientists from the ISRO. In keeping with this theme, STC invited two well-known scientists in the field of space technology for presentation. The first talk was delivered by Dr. B.S Munjal, Space Applications Centre (SAC), ISRO. Dr. Munjal spoke on "Technology Applications in Space/Ground Segment of SATCOM Domain". It was followed by the demonstration/display of technologies developed at IIT Kanpur. A group of school children from the city participated in the event.

Supported Areas of Research

Research proposals are Supported by ISRO in any area of relevance to the space programme of which the following are few examples:

Space Science: Physics of the ionosphere and magnetosphere; meteorology, dynamics of the atmosphere; geophysics, geology; astronomy; cosmology; astrophysics; planetary and interplanetary space physics and climatology.

Space Technology: Rocket and satellite technology; propulsion systems design and optimization; aerodynamics and heat transfer problems related to space vehicles; guidance and control systems for launch vehicles and spacecraft; polymer chemistry, propellant technology; ultra-light-weight structure; satellite energy systems; space electronics, Space communication systems; orbital mechanics ,computer sciences and new material development.

[Context: ISRO-IITK SPACE TECHNOLOGY CELL (STC), IIT KANPUR > STC page: /stc/research-areas.htm]
Space Application: Remote sensing of the earth’s resources: space communication; satellite geodesy image processing, satellite meteorology including weather forecasting, Space Education and Ecology.

Some illustrative examples of specific problems in the above-mentioned areas can be made available by ISRO from time to time by sharing the titles of the old projects - list of projects from 2000 - July 2023. Proposers need not feel to be restricted in the selection of subjects, research areas etc for the proposals, but they need to demonstrate the intrinsic scientific merit. However, ISRO reserves the right to not to fund any proposal (s), if in its opinion, similar proposals are already funded or similar work is already done in ISRO.

Sponsored Research at STC  |

Procedure for New Proposal

Step 1: |

Proposal prepared in standard Format by PI  (Principle Investigator).

Step 2: |

Proposal received at STC, IIT Kanpur.

Step 3: |

Proposal referred to DD RESPOND, ISRO, HQ.

Step 4: |

Proposal referred to ISRO Subject Experts.

Step 5: |

Comments received from ISRO Subject Experts.

Step 6: |

If ISRO Subject Experts find any kind of modification, PI will  prepare the revised Proposal
and send it to STC (Step 1  to Step 4). This process is in loop till SSE is satisfied and then proposal is  discussed in  JPC(Joint Proof Review Committe )
[Context: ISRO-IITK SPACE TECHNOLOGY CELL (STC), IIT KANPUR > STC page: /stc/procedure-new.htm]
.

Step 7: |

If JPC members find any kind of modification, again PI will prepare the
revised Proposal and send  it to  STC (Step 1 to Step 5). This  process is in loop till JPC clearance.

Step 8: |

After clearance  by JPC, Director IITK recommend  the proposal for approval by ISRO.

Step 9: |

Approval of Chairman, ISRO.

Step 10: |

A letter from DD, RESPOND will be sent to PI on approval intimation and further procedure for release of grant.

Step 11: |

STC Coordinator will Grant-in-aid bill for all the projects to Pay and Accounts Officer, Department of Space, Antariksh Bhavan, New BEL Road, Bangalore 560 231  with a copy to DD, RESPOND (Department of Space, Antariksh Bhavan, New BEL Road, Bangalore 560 231)  for releasing the grants.

[Context: ISRO-IITK SPACE TECHNOLOGY CELL (STC), IIT KANPUR > STC page: /stc/procedure-new.htm]

Step 12: |

The release of approved ISRO grants to the project is subject to the submission of all the outstanding  Fund Utilization Certificate (FUC) and Audited Accounts Statement (AAS) by institution, for the ISRO grants released earlier to the institution for conducting ISRO sponsored projects and other scientific activities.

Step 13: |

DOS will release order for the approved grants followed by release of fund to Institute.
