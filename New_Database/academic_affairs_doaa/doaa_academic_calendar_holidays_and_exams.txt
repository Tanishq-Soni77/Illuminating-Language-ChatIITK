To add this calendar to your personal calendar-

Calendar Uniform Resource Locators (URL links):

ICAL:https://calendar.google.com/calendar/ical/5fv2ggkjesmp80nkd6q4f3e1es%40group.calendar.google.com/public/basic.ics

HTML:https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=5fv2ggkjesmp80nkd6q4f3e1es%40group.calendar.google.com&ctz=Asia/Calcutta

For those using google calendar:

In the google calendar, on the left hand side of the  screen, you have an item called "My calendars"  and then an item called "Other calendars".

Next to "Other calendars" there is a drop down menu  button. If you click there, you see five options to  see other calendars. One of them is "Add by URL"  Click on that.
