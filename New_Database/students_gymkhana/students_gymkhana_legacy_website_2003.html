=== Student festivals (2003-04) ===

Source: https://www.iitk.ac.in/gymkhana/festivals.html

On behalf of the institute the students gymkhana organizes the following student festivals. All festivals are an annnual feature and finds huge student participation both, within and from other colleges.

ANTARAGNI : It is a inter-collegiate cultural festival in which students from all over india participate. It has been scheduled for the month of october-november 2003.

UDGHOSH : On similar lines of antaragni, udghosh is a national level sports meet. It has been scheduled for the month of november 2003.

UMANG : It is a week long SFS festival where major blockbusters and award winning movies are screened on the big screen. It has been scheduled for january 2004.

[Context: STUDENTS' GYMKHANA WEBSITE - LEGACY PAGES FROM 2003-04 (ARCHIVED, OUTDATED) > Student festivals (2003-04)]

TECHKRITI : It is the annual Technological Festival of IIT Kanpur. Techkriti provides the forum for engineering talent to exhibit applications and devices, along with attending various workshops and lectures. Overrall, there is a tremendous opportunity for knowledge sharing and cross-fertilization of ideas. It has been scheduled for february 2004.

GALAXY : It is an institute level event where the various halls of residences vie for the coveted "Best Hall Of The Year" award. It has been scheduled for march-april 2004.

MEGABUCKS : It is an intercollegiate/international business plan event for the student community.

=== Students' Senate (2003-04) ===

Source: https://www.iitk.ac.in/gymkhana/senate.html

The Students' Senate is the main legislative and advisory body of Student's Gymkhana. It comprises of the executives and the senators elected from the general body of Students' Gymkhana. Presently, there are 29 members (5 executives and 24 senators).

Source: https://www.iitk.ac.in/gymkhana/constitution.html

PREAMBLE

For the purpose of following a corporate life based on equality of status and sound moral judgement; developing all round extra-curricular activities to supplement education; and advancing the general welfare of our fellow students;We, the Students of Indian Institute of Technology, Kanpur resolve to constitute a self governing democratic organization called Student's Gymkhana and do hereby adopt this Constitution.
