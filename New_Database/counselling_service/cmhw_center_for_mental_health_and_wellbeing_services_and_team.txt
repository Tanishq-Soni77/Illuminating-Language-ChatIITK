Annual Events

2025 - 2026

Suicide Prevention Day (Ikigai)

Sep 10

World Suicide Prevention Day is observed on 10 September every year to promote global awareness and collective action to prevent suicide. Since 2003, this day has been marked worldwide through various initiatives aimed at highlighting that suicide is preventable and at reducing the stigma surrounding mental health.

This year, the event was titled “Ikigai”, a Japanese concept meaning “the value or purpose of life.” Activities included:

Board Games Night

Talk show featuring:

Dr. Alok Bajpai (Psychiatrist)

Dr. Geeta Singh (Psychiatrist)

Mr. Aniket Kumar (Civil Servant)

T-shirt Painting Activity

[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/events.php]
Mental Health Week

Oct 19

On the occasion of World Mental Health Day, the Center for Mental Health and Well-being organized a series of inspiring events aimed at promoting mental well-being and raising awareness about the importance of mental health. The initiatives focused on encouraging open conversations.

The session concluded with a movie screening of “Dear Zindagi” .

Hakuna Matata

Oct 20

Every year on Diwali, the Institute Counselling Service organizes “Hakuna Matata”, a celebration creating a sense of togetherness for students away from home. Theme: “Home Away from Home”.

Games Night & Jamming Night

Diya and Rangoli Painting Competition

Distribution of sweets by SIS guards

Lumos Maxima – art installation using diyas

[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/events.php]
Lantern Flying Event

Run and Walk for a Cause

Oct 21

A 5km Run and Walk event to spirit up the mental health day and bring attention to the importance of physical and mental health. The Marathon started at OAT and continued through the Campus. Goodies and energy drinks were distributed to all participants.

Dialogues

Every Month

Dialogues is an interactive event designed to bring students together in a safe and open environment to discuss mental health and wellness. It provides a platform where individuals can share experiences, exchange perspectives, and engage in meaningful conversations. Through open dialogue, the event aims to promote awareness, understanding, and a supportive community.

Workshops

Trauma Sensitization

26 December 2025

The Centre for Mental Health and Wellbeing (CMHW) hosted an online Trauma Sensitization Workshop aimed at building awareness and understanding of trauma through a transdiagnostic perspective. The session was facilitated by Ms. Arpita Roy, Chartered Clinical Psychologist (UK), M.Phil. in Clinical Psychology from NIMHANS (1995), and a former NHS (UK) Clinician with 16 years of experience in mental health services.

Date: 26 December 2025 | Time: 10:00 AM - 1:00 PM | Duration: 3 hours | Free of charge.

Develop a basic understanding of trauma and complex trauma

Understand the long-term effects of trauma across the lifespan

[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/workshops.php]

Learn how trauma-informed thinking can guide supportive and therapeutic work

Sensitization Workshop on Mental Health & Suicide Prevention

Oct 01-02, 2025

The Centre for Mental Health and Wellbeing (CMHW) conducted a Sensitization Workshop on Mental Health and Suicide Prevention, held on 1st and 2nd October 2025. The workshop aimed to raise awareness about mental health issues and equip participants with essential knowledge and skills for suicide prevention and crisis response.

Understanding mental health and common psychological challenges

Awareness of warning signs and risk factors associated with suicide

Practical guidance on how to respond to someone in crisis

What if I arrive before 21st July?

You will have to reside outside IITK as your hostel room will be allotted only on 21st July.

When should we report to campus?

Please note that the date of reporting to campus is 21st July from 7:00 am- 7:30 pm, and rooms will be allocated on arrival itself. Students are very strongly recommended to reach on 21st July, otherwise, they will miss a part of their Orientation.  Orientation will be from 22nd July onwards. Parents Orientation is scheduled on 22nd July itself.

[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/orientation.php]
When will I receive IIT Kanpur Email ID and Roll Number?

You will receive an e-mail containing your Roll Number and IIT Kanpur Email ID before the reporting date.

What is Pingala Portal?

Pingala is a comprehensive online portal for IIT Kanpur, designed to streamline administrative, academic, and procedural requirements. Avoid logging in before getting credentials, otherwise the account will be locked.The details of account creation in Pingala will be communicated by Dean of Digital Infrastructure & Automation (DDIA) office before your arrival.

Are parents required in the orientation/registration process?

No, parents are not required in any step of the orientation/registration process. However, there is a Parents Orientation planned for 22nd July which the parents are encouraged to attend.

What things to bring to IITK?

Here is a pdf containing a suggestive list of items to bring to IITK: Items to Bring

Are Parents allowed to stay on campus?

Parents can visit their ward’s allocated hall. They must vacate the Hall at 7:30 pm at night. They must make arrangements for their lodging themself. It's recommended that parents make arrangements in advance.

Will Digi-locker Documents work?

Digilocker files will work for online uploading and verification. But for offline on-campus document verification, we require original tangible documents.

Does any repeater have to upload extra documents?

No. He has to upload the same files as the rest.

From where I can get the details of fees to be paid by me?

[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/orientation.php]
Approved fee structure is available at your Pingala web page and DoSA website under the head 'News and Events' 'For New Students'.

I had applied for Bank Loan, how I can get the fee structure for the whole course?

Tentative fee certificate for new batch of B.Tech/BS/MSc is available in DoSA website under the head 'News and Events' 'For New Students'. However, if you require semester wise fee details, please write to dosarecep@iitk.ac.in.

What kind of fee remissions are allowed?

The fees waiver are given to the following category of students:
- MEB: Most Economically Backward families (income < ₹1 lakh) get 100% tuition waiver.
- OEB: Other Economically Backward families (income ₹1–5 lakh) get 1/3rd tuition fee.
[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/orientation.php]
- SC/ST/PWD: Fully exempted from tuition based on category certificate.
No waiver for M.Sc./Ph.D/MBA/M.Des/DIIT students except SC/ST/PWD category.

What is the valid income proof for fee remission/waiver for BS/BT students?

Copy of complete Income Tax Return (ITR) of family (Self, Father, Mother, Unmarried Siblings) and Tuition Fee Waiver Affidavit on Rs. 20/- Stamp paper duly signed by First Class Magistrate/Public Notary as per format available on DoSA website. Submit original affidavit to DoSA office in first semester.

Whether the EWS certificate can be considered valid for fee remission for BS/BT students?

No. EWS certificate alone is not considered. Income proof must comply with Para 4(a).

[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/orientation.php]
If a student fails to submit valid income proof at registration?

The student shall deposit full fees at registration and excess fees may be claimed on submission of valid income certificate to DoSA office.

What to do if name mismatch between Institute records and provided income certificate?

Submit Request Letter, Publication of names in Gazette/News Papers for reconsideration of income certificates.

On what grounds can I get refund of excess fee amounts?

Excess fees can be refunded on submission of valid proof within one year through Pingala Portal.

What is the procedure to apply for refund of excess fee?

Apply in Pingala Portal in Excess Fee Refund Module.

I have paid my all fees but Pingala shows 'Not Registered'?

Reasons: a) Scrolling issue on Pingala portal b) Online payment reconciliation c) Payment via RTGS – send UTR to receipts@iitk.ac.in, brsac1@iitk.ac.in, dosaregd@iitk.ac.in. Also check for pending dues and biometric attendance.

How to claim Fee Waiver/Refund as a newly joined student?

1. Upload income certificate in Pingala portal after roll allotment.
2. After DoSA approval, submit excess fee refund form with payment slip through Pingala portal.
3. Refund sent through Account section or TSA account.

Who is eligible for fee refund?

a) Paid fee twice.
b) Paid excess fee due to missing Income certificate.
c) Late Day Scholar approval.
d) State scholarships paid directly.
e) Any other case-specific excess fees.

Procedure for education loan fee clearance?

Bank transfers payment to IIT Kanpur SBI account. Collect advise note from bank and send to receipts@iitk.ac.in, brsac1@iitk.ac.in, dosaregd@iitk.ac.in. For tentative fees, write to dosaregd@iitk.ac.in.

Will my EWS certificate be applicable for Fee waiver?

No, EWS certificate is not applicable for fee waiver.

Do I need to file ITR if I want fee remission and have never filed ITR?

It is mandatory for parents to submit their ITR. ITR can be filed online. Student or siblings submit if asked.

If I have income certificate and affidavit, is ITR/AIS still required?

Yes, ITR + AIS from Income Tax Department is mandatory to receive fee remission.

Is PAN Card compulsory?

PAN Card is mandatory for filing ITR. If zero income, declare PAN number and zero income. If parents don’t have PAN, they must apply for it.

What is AIS/TIS?

AIS (Annual Information Statement) is a detailed statement from Income Tax Department. TIS is condensed version. AIS required along with ITR.

What scholarships are available through IITK?

How can I apply for eligible scholarships?

Notification sent to students. Submit forms to SSPC Office via email (sspc@iitk.ac.in) or by hand at SSPC Office.

Process for signature on External Scholarships?

Submit duly signed forms by applicant & parents to SSPC Office either in hardcopy or scanned manner (sspc@iitk.ac.in). Attach fee receipt of 1st semester and grade sheets till 2nd semester of previous academic year.

When should I apply for scholarships as a first-year student?

First announcement mail sent after semester starts.

Yes, apply every academic year separately.

Can I get Bonafide certificate for scholarship from SSPC?

Yes, only for scholarship purposes.

What kind of no-objection certificates related to scholarships are issued?

Only scholarships related to NOC are issued by SSPC.

Reaching IITK

[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/orientation.php]
By Air

The most popular air route to IIT Kanpur is through Lucknow Airport (IATA: LKO | ICAO: VILK). Lucknow is connected by direct flights to and from major cities like Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata and Bengaluru

Note: Kanpur airport (Chakeri) is now open to civilian purpose. The Airport is located around 21 Km from IITK and cabs are available to and fro.

By Road

Kanpur is connected to NH-2 that runs between Amritsar and Kolkata and connecting cities like Delhi, Agra, Allahabad, Patna. Kanpur is also connected to Lucknow on NH25 and is about 91 km from Lucknow. Cabs are available from Lucknow to reach IIT Kanpur.

By Train

Kanpur is well connected by railway network with nearly every major city. Numerous overnight trains are also present from major cities like New Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore. IIT Kanpur is about 17 km from the Kanpur Central Railway Station.

[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/orientation.php]
Direct buses to campus front gate are there from Jhakarkati. Tempos and Autos are also available which take about Rs. 200 - Rs. 250 from Station to IITK campus. One can also find shared auto services (commonly known as Vikram) for as low as 40 Rupees from Kanpur Railway Station, outside Platform 9 to campus front gate.

Metro services are also available from Kanpur Central Metro Station to IIT Kanpur Metro Station from 6am to 10pm daily.

Individual Therapy

Individual therapy offers a safe, confidential one-on-one space to discuss personal, emotional, academic, or psychological concerns, with empathetic guidance from trained counsellors through free, flexible, and personalized support.

Regular Session
Single Session Therapy (In-Person)
Single Session Therapy (Online)

Regular Counselling Session

Regular Counselling Session involve ongoing one-on-one meetings with a professional counsellor over a period of time. These session are designed for individuals who wish to work more deeply on their concerns and benefit from consistent support, guidance, and follow-up.

[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/individual-therapy.php]
Regular session provide a safe and confidential space to explore emotional, academic, interpersonal, or psychological issues in depth. The counsellor works collaboratively with the client to understand patterns, past experiences (when relevant), and current challenges, and helps develop coping strategies and skills that can be practiced between session.

This form of therapy is especially helpful for long-standing concerns, recurring difficulties, or situations that require sustained professional guidance. The frequency and duration of session are decided jointly by the client and the counsellor, based on individual needs and progress.

Regular counselling allows for continuity, trust-building, and structured growth over time, enabling individuals to work toward long-term well-being and personal development.

[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/individual-therapy.php]
Single Session Therapy (Online)

Single Session Therapy (Online) is a one-time, one-on-one counselling session conducted through a secure online platform. It is designed for those seeking timely support from their own space without a long-term commitment.

How Does it Work?

Online SST focuses on addressing a specific concern within a single interaction. Your therapist will collaborate with you to:

Understand the concern clearly.

Explore thoughts, emotions, and available options.

Identify personal strengths and coping strategies.

Formulate practical steps to work on the concern independently.

Eligibility & Expectations

Duration |
Minimum 30 mins (depending on the concern). |

Platform |
Online only via a secure platform. |

[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/individual-therapy.php]
Availability |
Appointments booked Mon–Thu; Session held during CMHW office hours (Mon–Fri). |

Focus |
Best suited for one clearly defined or short concern. |

Follow-up |
Clients can re-book after two weeks; previous session must be disclosed. |

All services are free of cost and completely confidential. Information is kept private except where required by ethical or legal obligations.

Important Note: Online SST is not recommended for long-standing concerns or severe mental health conditions. For recurring issues, please book Regular Counselling Session for sustained professional care.

Single Session Therapy (In-Person)

Single Session Therapy (In-Person) is a one-time, face-to-face counselling session conducted at the counselling Center. It is designed for individuals who prefer in-person interaction and seek immediate support for a specific concern without a long-term commitment.

[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/individual-therapy.php]

In-person SST focuses on addressing your present concern within a single in-person interaction. Your therapist will work with you to:

Understand the concern in detail.

Explore thoughts, emotions, and possible perspectives.

Clarify doubts and uncertainties.

Identify personal strengths and coping resources.

Provide practical guidance or action steps for independent progress.

1. About the SBF Scholarship

The Students’ Benevolent Fund (SBF) Scholarship is a financial assistance program at IIT Kanpur designed to support students who are experiencing financial hardship and are not beneficiaries of any other scholarship or fellowship.

Key Features

Monthly assistance of approximately ₹2,000

Disbursed for 9 months in an academic year

Can be availed for a maximum of 3 years

Not contingent upon a prescribed CPI cutoff

[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/sbf.php]
Students may reapply each academic year, subject to eligibility

2. Eligibility Criteria

The SBF Scholarship is open to:

Undergraduate (UG) students

MSc students

Dual Degree students

Important Notes

Students currently receiving any other scholarship or fellowship are not eligible

Acceptance of SBF may require return of other financial assistance, if applicable

Dual Degree students transitioning to MTech/MS programs are not eligible

3. Application Timeline & Process

The application process is tentatively expected to begin around 3rd January, 2026

Applications will be submitted through the Student Academic Portal

Only complete and accurate applications will be considered

Incomplete, incorrect, or misleading submissions may lead to cancellation of the application.

Student Documents

Student’s PAN Card

Family / Parent Documents (Mandatory)

Parents’ PAN Card (Mandatory even if the parent is a homemaker or non-earning)

Family Annual Income Certificate
OR

Parents’ Income Tax Return (ITR) / AIS

Other Sources of Income

Supporting documents must be provided for any additional income, including pension, rental, agricultural, business, or other income sources, as applicable.

Important Clarifications

PAN Card and AIS/ITR are mandatory in all cases

Homemaker or non-earning status does not exempt submission of PAN or AIS/ITR

What is Counselling and Psychotherapy?

Counselling and psychotherapy are collaborative processes that help individuals understand and address personal, emotional, academic, interpersonal and mental health concerns. They provide a safe, confidential, and non-judgmental space to explore thoughts, feelings, and experiences, develop effective coping strategies, and work toward personal goals.

Counselling often focuses on current concerns, life challenges, and specific goals, such as stress, relationship difficulties, adjustment issues, academic concerns, or decision-making. Psychotherapy typically involves a deeper exploration of thoughts, emotions, behaviours and long-standing patterns that may contribute to distress. It may be helpful for more persistent or complex difficulties. In practice, there is considerable overlap between counselling and psychotherapy, and the approach used will depend on your individual needs and goals. If needed, you may also be referred to another mental health professional or healthcare provider who can best support your needs.

[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/faq.php]
Do I have to pay for Counselling?

No. All registered students (undergraduate and postgraduate), employees of the institute and their registered dependents can avail the service free of cost. The expenditure involved if you wish to see the psychiatrist is also borne by the service.

How do I avail the service?

You can book an appointment through the website homepage and choose between offline sessions and online Single Session Therapy (SST) for initial support. If you are experiencing significant distress and would like to speak with a counsellor sooner, you may walk in to the CMHW office during working hours (10:00 AM-9:00 PM, Monday-Friday). For urgent concerns outside office hours, please visit the Health Centre, where arrangements can be made to connect you with an on-call counsellor.

[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/faq.php]
What are the facilities available?

CMHW offers the following facilities:

• Offline counselling sessions

• Online Single Session Therapy (SST)

• 24x7 telephonic mental health support through Tele-MANAS (Government of India helpline)

Apart from the above CMHW also provides clinical facilities such as psychiatrists and cyber support.

What are Offline counselling sessions?

Offline counselling refers to face-to-face sessions with a counsellor at the CMHW's office. These sessions are accessible between 10:00 AM and 9:00 PM (Monday-Friday) for better convenience and support.

What are Online Single Session Therapy (SST) sessions?

Online Single Session Therapy (SST) refers to a one-time, focused therapy session conducted online with a qualified counsellor. These sessions aim to address immediate concerns and provide coping strategies, and are available by appointment.

[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/faq.php]
What is an Anonymous Referral?

An anonymous referral allows you to share concerns about someone who may be experiencing emotional distress, mental health difficulties, or other challenges affecting their well-being, without revealing your identity. If a referral is received, the CMHW team may reach out to the individual to inform them about available support services and offer appropriate support. If there is an immediate concern about someone's safety or well-being, please contact the Health Centre or the Head, CMHW directly rather than relying solely on an anonymous referral.

What kind of problems can I talk to a counsellor about?

You can talk to a counsellor about any concern that is affecting your emotional well-being, daily functioning, relationships, academic or work life. Some common concerns include:

[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/faq.php]
• Stress, anxiety, or low mood

• Academic or workplace challenges

• Relationship and family difficulties

• Adjustment to a new environment or life changes

• Loneliness, homesickness, or social concerns

• Self-esteem and confidence issues

• Grief, loss, or other difficult life experiences

• Emotional distress, overwhelming feelings, or mental health concerns

You do not need to be in a crisis to seek counselling. If something is bothering you or affecting your well-being, you are welcome to reach out for support.

What are Tele-MANAS sessions?

Tele MANAS (Tele Mental Health Assistance and Networking Across States) is India's 24/7, free tele-mental health initiative providing accessible counseling, consultation, e-prescriptions, and referrals via a toll-free helpline (14416) and app, aiming to offer comprehensive mental health care to all citizens, especially vulnerable groups, in multiple languages. It's a digital extension of the National Mental Health Programme (NMHP) designed to bridge gaps in mental healthcare.

What is Mindcheck?

MindCheck is a mental wellness and self-reflection mobile application developed by the TRANSiT Lab in association with CMHW at IIT Kanpur. Designed to provide a quiet space for daily self-reflection rather than diagnosis, the app helps users stay in touch with their overall well-being.

Key features include:

• Guided mental wellness check-ins

• Mood tracking and journaling

• Self-assessment questionnaires and cognitive games

• Wellbeing trends tracking

• Quick access to institute support resources (such as CMHW counselling, YourDOST, TreadWill, and Health Centre emergency contacts)

It operates as a Progressive Web App (PWA) that can be accessed via a browser link and added directly to your mobile home screen.

What is the difference between a Psychologist, Clinical Psychologist, and Psychiatrist?

Psychologists have training in Psychology and provide counseling and psychotherapy for a wide range of emotional, personal, interpersonal, and academic concerns. Clinical Psychologists have specialized training in the assessment, diagnosis, and psychological treatment of mental health conditions. They may also conduct psychological assessments when required. Psychiatrists are medical doctors who specialize in mental health. They can assess and diagnose mental health conditions, prescribe medications when needed and work collaboratively with psychologists and clinical psychologists. You do not need to decide which professional to see. Based on your concerns, CMHW will help connect you with the most appropriate professional.

[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/faq.php]
What will the counsellor think of me?

Counsellors are trained to listen with empathy, respect, and without judgment. People seek counselling for many different reasons and there is no concern that is too small or too unusual to discuss. The purpose of counselling is not to judge, criticize or tell you what to do. Instead, counsellors work with you to better understand your experiences, explore possible solutions, and support you in making decisions that are right for you. Many people seek counselling at different points in their lives, and reaching out for support is a normal and positive step toward improving well-being.

How confidential is counselling?

Counselling is a confidential service. Information shared during counselling is not disclosed to others without your knowledge and consent. There are, however, exceptional circumstances where confidentiality may need to be limited to protect your safety or the safety of others, or when disclosure is required by law. Examples include situations involving a risk of serious harm to yourself or others, concerns about abuse or neglect, or compliance with legal requirements. Whenever possible, the counsellor will discuss the situation with you before sharing any information and will disclose only what is necessary.

[Context: CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING (CMHW), IIT KANPUR - SERVICES, TEAM, APPOINTMENTS AND SU > Counselling page: /cmhw/pages/faq.php]
If I have had counselling, does it go on my record?

No. Counselling is a completely confidential service and does not go on your academic or personal record. The counsellor will keep brief notes for their own use, but these are not part of any official record and are destroyed after a certain period of time.

Who are Academic Mentors?

Academic Mentors are senior students selected to assist you with specific courses. They hold regular remedial sessions and can help clear doubts regarding course material and exam preparation.

What is Central Academic Directory?

You can access the Central Academic Directory via the Resources tab in the menu. This repository contains notes, past papers, and books curated by our team.

What are AM classes?

AM classes are remedial sessions conducted by Academic Mentors to help students grasp difficult concepts and prepare for exams effectively. Remedial classes are just a help provided when there is difficulty in grasping the concepts in college lectures. They may not cover the entire syllabus (only the difficult parts) and are sometimes not as regular as college lectures.

How do I get in contact with Academic Mentors?

For first-year courses, the list of student names is available on the team page under Academic Mentors tab . For all other courses, you may contact any team member listed on the team page under Core Team Members, Academics tab, and they will connect you with a suitable Academic Mentor (AM)

Anytime, when the mentor is free. It depends on mutual understanding between the mentor and student. The mentor can be contacted via phone or other means, and unless he/she is in the middle of something very important, he/she is willing to help out

What should I do if I don't know English well?

For students who are not proficient in English, we offer English Communication Classes (ECC), which are conducted as one-on-one mentoring sessions with a professional language instructor. These sessions are designed to help students build confidence, improve communication skills, and become more comfortable with the language at their own pace.

In addition, we ensure a calm, supportive, and non-judgmental environment where students are encouraged to learn without pressure. Our mentors focus on making students feel relaxed, understood, and emotionally supported throughout the learning process.

Not at all! It is always possible to miss out on certain concepts while in class or during self study and the academic load may make us all feel pressurized at times. Therefore, asking for help doesn't undermine your intelligence or your capabilities, but only demonstrates your willingness to learn.

Can CMHW help in updating grades?

No, CMHW does not handle or assist with grade updates. For any concerns related to grades, students are advised to contact their respective academic department or the concerned faculty.
