CMHW SELF-ASSESSMENT TESTS (CENTER FOR MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLBEING, IIT KANPUR)
Name: Self-assessment tools offered on the CMHW website (https://www.iitk.ac.in/counsel/cmhw/pages/self-assessments.php).
Type: Online self-reflection tests, not clinical diagnoses. The six test pages themselves load their questions with JavaScript, so only their identifiers (test codes) are available: ghq-12 (General Health Questionnaire, 12 items), brs, internet-addiction, happiness-scale (Happiness Scale), mental-health-stigma (Mental Health Stigma Test) and gratitude-questionnaire (Gratitude Questionnaire). Results note: "This is a self-assessment tool designed for reflection. It is not a clinical diagnosis. If you have concerns about your mental health, please consult with a mental health professional at CMHW."
