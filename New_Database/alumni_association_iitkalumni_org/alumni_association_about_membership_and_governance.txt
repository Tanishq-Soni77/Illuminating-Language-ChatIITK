About Us

The Alumni Association is a vibrant network that connects alumni with their alma mater and fellow graduates across the world. It aims to strengthen lifelong relationships, encourage professional networking, support students through mentorship and guidance, and contribute to the growth of the institution.

Through reunions, events, webinars, and community initiatives, the Association fosters collaboration, shared experiences, and a strong spirit of giving back among alumni.

Alumni Chapters

Chapters work actively towards promoting networking among themselves and with the institute as well.

Reunions

Organizing alumni Reunions is an integral part of the Alumni Association of IITK.

President's Message

On behalf of the Alumni Association's Board of Directors and as President of the IIT Kanpur Alumni Association, I extend my warmest greetings to our vast and distinguished community.

[Context: IIT KANPUR ALUMNI ASSOCIATION - ABOUT, MEMBERSHIP, BOARD, CHAPTERS AND CONTACT > Alumni Association page: Home]
It is indeed an honor to represent a community of over 40,000 accomplished individuals who have left an indelible mark on various global fields. The diverse achievements of our alumni, spanning industries, academia, entrepreneurship, and public service, stand as a testament to the exceptional education and values instilled during our time at IIT Kanpur.

Our journey as IIT Kanpur alumni is characterized by a shared commitment to excellence, innovation, and societal impact. As we face new challenges and opportunities, I encourage each of you to actively engage with the association. Your participation can take various forms, whether it's joining alumni events, contributing to various programs, or sharing your expertise to enhance the legacy of our alma mater.

[Context: IIT KANPUR ALUMNI ASSOCIATION - ABOUT, MEMBERSHIP, BOARD, CHAPTERS AND CONTACT > Alumni Association page: Home]
Together, let us continue to uphold the legacy of excellence and innovation that defines IIT Kanpur. Your input and participation are invaluable in shaping the future of our association and strengthening the bonds that tie us together as proud IIT Kanpur alumni.

Thank you for your continued support, and I eagerly anticipate connecting with you at our upcoming events.

Warm regards,
Rakesh Sharma

Benefits of becoming the Life Member of the Alumni Association IIT Kanpur

Global Alumni Network

Join a worldwide network of IIT Kanpur alumni, connecting you to a diverse and influential community around the globe.

Institute-Alumni Bridge

The Alumni Association serves as a vital link between the Institute and its graduates, fostering collaboration and communication.

Stay Informed

Receive the IITK Konnect (IITK Newsletter) and other IITK publications to stay updated on the latest news and events at your alma mater.

Local Chapters

Access Alumni Chapters globally, participating in regular chapter get-togethers and exclusive events.

Special Institute Events

[Context: IIT KANPUR ALUMNI ASSOCIATION - ABOUT, MEMBERSHIP, BOARD, CHAPTERS AND CONTACT > Alumni Association page: /benefits]
Be invited to special events and ceremonies hosted by the Institute and Alumni Chapters worldwide.

Batch Reunions

Attend milestone batch/class reunions like the 15th, 25th, 30th, 35th, 40th, and 50th, with assistance from the Alumni Association in organizing other batch reunions upon request. Reunion photos and videos are made available through the website here after the events.

Digital Networking

Connect with alumni via PANIIT, Yahoo groups (batch/class-wise), Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter, and more.

Academic Services

Obtain your academic transcripts and other certificates conveniently through the Alumni Association.

Mentorship

Benefit from mentoring opportunities provided by senior alumni, including participation in SEE and SMC programs.

[Context: IIT KANPUR ALUMNI ASSOCIATION - ABOUT, MEMBERSHIP, BOARD, CHAPTERS AND CONTACT > Alumni Association page: /benefits]
Souvenir Shop

Explore the Alumni Association's own souvenir shop, where you can find memorabilia and merchandise celebrating your alma mater

Exclusive Email ID

Receive a permanent email ID (e.g., xxxxyop@iitkalumni.org) by sending a request to alumni_office@iitk.ac.in, providing your personal details, alumni ID, and more.

Credit Card Facility

Enjoy access to an exclusive credit card facility designed for alumni, offering competitive terms, customized designs, and rewards programs. Insurance Policy: Secure your financial future with a comprehensive insurance policy tailored to the needs of our alumni.

Campus Tours

The Alumni Association can assist in organizing campus tours for you and your family, allowing you to revisit and explore the campus.

[Context: IIT KANPUR ALUMNI ASSOCIATION - ABOUT, MEMBERSHIP, BOARD, CHAPTERS AND CONTACT > Alumni Association page: /benefits]
Campus Facilities

Enjoy discounted rates for alumni in the Visitors Hostel, book VH rooms at IITK, and access other campus facilities. Additionally, you can visit hostels, the library, departments, labs, and even meet with faculty members with the Alumni Association's help.

Privacy and Security

Rest assured that your life membership database is maintained securely by the Alumni Association and not shared with unauthorized parties. It also offers Degree Verification Services for pre- and post-employment screening companies.
